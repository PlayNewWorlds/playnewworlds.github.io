Privacy notice

New Worlds · last updated 6 October 2026

In short: you can play New Worlds without giving us anything. There are no accounts, no emails, no advertising and no tracking cookies. We only hold data about you if you turn on cloud saves or play multiplayer, and even then it is a random ID, your saves and what it takes to connect you to other players. You can delete all of it from inside the game.

Who we are

New Worlds is made by Thomas Ashley trading as computerise, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: privacy@computerise.games.

What other players see

Players in a session with you see your display name, your ship and its colour, where you are in the game, and what you do in their world. Multiplayer connects players directly, so the host and the guests connected to them can also see each other's IP address; guests don't connect to each other. Choose a display name that isn't your real name.

Other players' games remember you by your player ID, not your name: their saves, cloud saves and logs hold the ID, and look your name up from us when they show it (keeping a copy on their device so it still shows offline, refreshed whenever they're online). So if you change your name on the Multiplayer screen, they see the new one; if you delete your cloud data, your name is gone from us and they see "Former player" instead. Saves made by an older version of the game may still contain names until the game is updated and the save is written again.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare runs our server, database, relay and site analytics, and GitHub hosts the website. They act on our instructions under their data processing terms. Both may process data outside the UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards UK law requires for such transfers. We don't sell your data, show advertising, build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

Children

We don't ask for names, emails, ages or locations. Cloud saves are off by default and multiplayer is invite-only. If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before turning on cloud saves or joining multiplayer.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to have it in a portable form.

  • Delete everything: in the game, go to Settings → Profile → Delete my cloud data. It erases your player, every device on it and every cloud save at once. Saves on your device are kept.
  • Anything else, or if you've lost access to the game: email privacy@computerise.games with your Player ID (shown in Settings → Profile). We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change affects data we already hold, we'll say so in the game before it takes effect.
